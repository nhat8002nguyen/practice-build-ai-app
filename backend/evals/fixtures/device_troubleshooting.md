# Device Troubleshooting Guide

## Warranty

All devices include a 2 year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects.
Accidental damage and water damage are not covered.
To claim, contact support with your order number and a photo of the defect.

## Device Will Not Power On

Charge the device for at least 30 minutes using the original cable.
Hold the power button for 10 seconds to force a restart.
If the status light stays red, the battery needs replacement under warranty.

## Wi-Fi Connection Problems

Move the device within 5 meters of the router during setup.
Only 2.4 GHz networks are supported and 5 GHz networks are not.
Reset network settings by holding the setup button for 8 seconds until the light blinks blue.

## Firmware Updates

Firmware updates install automatically overnight when the device is idle and charging.
To update manually, open the app, select Device, and tap Check for updates.
Do not unplug the device while the light pulses white.

## Factory Reset

Q: How do I factory reset my device?
A: Hold the power and setup buttons together for 15 seconds. All paired accounts are removed.

Q: Does a factory reset delete my cloud data?
A: No. Cloud data stays in your account and syncs again after you sign in.
