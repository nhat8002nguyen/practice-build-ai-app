# Billing and Plans

## Plans

Starter costs 12 dollars per month and includes 3 team seats.
Pro costs 39 dollars per month and includes 15 team seats and priority support.
Enterprise pricing is custom and includes single sign-on and a dedicated account manager.

## Invoices

Invoices are emailed on the first day of each billing cycle.
You can download past invoices as PDF from Billing, then Invoice history.
To add a VAT number or company address, edit the Billing details before the invoice is issued.

## Payment Methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express.
Bank transfer is available for annual Pro and Enterprise plans.
A failed card payment is retried 3 times over 7 days before the account is paused.

## Subscription Refunds

Annual plans can be cancelled within 30 days of the first payment for a pro-rated refund.
Monthly plans are not refunded once the billing cycle has started.

## Cancelling

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Open Billing, choose Cancel plan, and confirm. Access continues until the end of the paid period.

Q: Will I lose my data when I cancel?
A: Your workspace data is kept for 60 days after cancellation and then permanently deleted.
