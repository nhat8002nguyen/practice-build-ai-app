# Acme Support Policy

## Refunds

Customers may request a full refund within 14 days of purchase.
Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 business days.
Digital goods that have been downloaded are not eligible for refunds.

## Shipping

Orders ship within 2 business days.
Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 business days.
Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs an extra 15 dollars.

## Account Help

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Use the reset link on the sign in page. The link expires after 30 minutes.

Q: Can I change my subscription plan?
A: Yes. Open Billing and choose a new plan. Changes apply at the next billing cycle.

Q: How do I contact a human agent?
A: Reply to any support email or use the Contact us form. We answer within 1 business day.
